Calculation basis

Film GSM = thickness (µm) × density (g/cm³).
Lamination glue is added once for each interface: Duplex = 1 interface, Triplex = 2.
Printed film adds the entered ink GSM.
Roll length from diameter uses the annulus formula and the total physical film thickness.
Production costing applies the entered waste percentage to material, ink and glue consumption.
